### 🛡️ OpenType Safety Features Built-In

* **Slashed Zero (`cv08`)**: Differentiates numeral `0` from capital `O` (preventing lethal 10 mg vs 1O mg dosage errors).
* **Curved Foot `l` (`cv05`)**: Separates lowercase `l` from numeral `1` and uppercase `I`.
* **Bilobe Serifs (`ss02`)**: Guarantees capital `I` cannot be mistaken for numeral `1` in biomarker formulas.
* **Crossbar `Z` (`cv11`)**: Decisively distinguishes letter `Z` from numeral `2`.
* **Philocardia Healer Tittles (`ss07` / `cv09`)**: Rounded heart fillets on lowercase `i`, `j`, and `!` to eliminate acute visual threat stimuli and soothe bedside anxiety.
