## 📖 Chapter 1: The Origin Story (GearArts Papercraft)

Pocket Gull began with an authentic hand-inked drawing on a physical cardstock postcard created for the **GearArts Pocket Gull** mark. The warm, organic curvature and calligraphic felt-pen stroke variation established the visual DNA of the typeface:

<div align="center">
  <img src="./geararts_pocketgull_origin_card.jpg" alt="GearArts Origin Card" width="85%" />
</div>

<br/>

From these 10 original hand-drawn vector paths (`P`, `o`, `c`, `k`, `e`, `t`, `G`, `u`, `l`, `l`), we extrapolated an entire superfamily spanning Fineliner (400), Bold (700), Chiseltip (900), Slab (400/700), and Mono (600 UPM fixed advance).

---

## 📐 Chapter 2: Libre Functionalism & Design Balance

Pocket Gull was built under the principles of **Libre Functionalist Design** (*Weniger, aber besser* / Less, but better) and careful human-centered engineering:

1. **Good Design is Honest (*Veritas*)**: No artificial 3D skewing or deceptive font masking. Every letter is true to its $1000\text{ UPM}$ TrueType grid.
2. **Good Design is as Little Design as Possible (*Simplicitas*)**: Clean Bézier curves, zero autotrace noise, and functional $45^\circ$ origami chamfers.
3. **Good Design Makes a Product Useful (*Aequabilitas*)**: Universal legibility calibrated to **WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast certification** and thoughtful character disambiguation (`0` vs `O`, `1` vs `l`, `7` vs `T`).
