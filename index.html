40.0 UPM (r = 13.5 UPM)

2. Screen Angle Geometry
45.0° (V1 Visual Cortex Angle)

The 45° angle matches the human visual cortex orientation columns where eye sensitivity to grid stepping is minimized. Eliminates horizontal/vertical stair-stepping artifacts.

For chromatic print stacking: Black/Outline: 45°, Magenta/Base: 75°, Cyan/Halftone: 15°, Yellow/Shadow: 0°. Standard 30° spacing prevents rosette collapse and moiré beating.

💡 Architectural Principle: Why Pure Direct Curves Outperform Bytecode Hints

TrueType hinting was created in 1989 for monochrome CRT monitors with 72 DPI resolutions, where a letter stem was only 1 or 2 physical pixels wide. In 2026, healthcare devices feature Retina, OLED, and 4K displays (264 to 450+ PPI). On modern high-density screens, subpixel antialiasing (ClearType, DirectWrite, FreeType v40) renders quadratic Bézier curves with surgical fidelity without manual pixel grid-snapping.

By declaring a Version 1 gasp table (0xFFFF = GASP_DOGRAY | GASP_SYMMETRIC_SMOOTHING) and passing all curves through clean quadratic conversion without bytecode instructions, PocketGull delivers instant zero-latency rasterization, eliminates font parser security vulnerabilities, and cuts display rendering battery consumption across mobile medical carts.

Multi-Script Typographic Principles Respecting Each Writing Tradition's Native Structure

• Let Latin be Latin: Crisp, disambiguated (ISMP), humanist, and readable.
• Let Arabic be Arabic: Honor the authentic reed-pen angle and baseline flow rather than artificial geometric flattening.
• Let Braille be Braille: Preserve standard tactile cell geometries and negative space (ISO/TR 11548 & ISO 17049).
• Let Canadian Syllabics be Syllabics: Respect rotational symmetry, optical stroke weights, and stroke terminals.
• Let Stenography be Stenography: Respect phonemic scale ratios without forcing shorthand into Roman letter proportions.
