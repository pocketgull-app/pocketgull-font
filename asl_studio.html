⚠️ Critical Disambiguation

• A vs. S vs. T: In A, thumb rests upright alongside index. In S, thumb folds across knuckles. In T, thumb pokes between index and middle.

• D vs. 1: In ASL, numeral 1 and letter D share the upright index, but D forms a closed circular loop with the other fingers and thumb.

• K vs. P: K points upright; P points downward toward the floor.
