PocketGull is an expressive, humanist sans-serif, clinical display, and telemetry monospace font superfamily engineered by Phil Gear.

Designed for both high-impact display branding and fatigue-resistant clinical reading, PocketGull bridges tactile humanist warmth with Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypes, full 256-glyph ISO tactile Braille, and Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP) character disambiguation to eliminate misreading and soothe reader fatigue across digital EHR workstations, medical summaries, and ICU telemetry displays.

To contribute, see github.com/pocketgull-app/pocketgull-font.
