▸ View Mathematical & Algorithmic Derivation + Barbara Oakley Key Chunk
1. OpenType MATH & Knuth METAFONT Stem Floor: Standard academic serifs thin nested sub/superscripts linearly, dropping stems below 1 device pixel on dark screens. PocketGull enforces a $68\text{ UPM}$ stem floor with `ScriptPercentScaleDown = 73%` and `ScriptScriptPercentScaleDown = 58%`.
2. Contiguous 600 UPM Box Topology: By locking every box-drawing glyph (`U+2500`–`U+257F`) to exact $[0, 600]\times[-180, 780]\text{ UPM}$ boundaries, tree edges connect with zero subpixel drift at any zoom level.
