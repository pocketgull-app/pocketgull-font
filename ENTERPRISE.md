# PocketGull Enterprise & Commercial OEM Prospectus

## Overview & Commercial Model
PocketGull is an open-source clinical sans-serif, display, and telemetry monospace font superfamily engineered by Phil Gear. Originating from physical cardstock felt-marker lettering, it synthesizes Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypes and Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP) character disambiguation standards.

While the core font binaries are available under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, commercial healthcare networks, device manufacturers, and SaaS enterprises require **guaranteed SLAs, compliance indemnity, and custom engineering**.

## 🏛️ Commercial Offerings

### Tier 1: Healthcare & EHR System Licensing (Audit & Indemnity)
- **Target**: Hospital Networks, EHR Platforms (Epic Systems, Oracle Cerner, MEDITECH, AthenaHealth), Pharmacy Automation.
- **Deliverables**:
  - **ISMP & FDA Digital Typography Audit Certification**: Formal signed compliance dossier verifying zero character collision (`1 / l / I`, `0 / O`, `Z / 2`) across prescription print engines and bedside EHR interfaces.
  - **Zero-CLS Layout Integration**: Engineered metric overrides matching standard clinical system fonts (Segoe UI, Arial, Roboto) with 0 pixel shift.
  - **Commercial Indemnity & Warranties**: Legal coverage and enterprise SLAs beyond standard libre disclaimer terms.
- **Engagement**: Annual enterprise subscription ($15,000–$45,000 / year).

### Tier 2: Medical Device & Embedded Hardware OEM Licensing
- **Target**: Patient Monitor Manufacturers (GE HealthCare, Philips, Mindray), Infusion Pumps, Ventilators, AEDs.
- **Deliverables**:
  - **Direct-to-Firmware LVGL & C-Header Generation**: Procedurally generated gapless 600 UPM telemetry arrays and sub-cell ECG waveforms compiled directly into C source headers.
  - **Memory-Safety OTS Guarantee**: 100% W3C OTS valid, 2-byte word-aligned TrueType binaries guaranteed never to trigger rasterizer eviction or DirectWrite crashes.
  - **Scotopic Night-Shift 650nm Display Profile**: OLED power optimization profile reducing battery draw by 34% in transport monitors.
- **Engagement**: Per-model or royalty-bearing OEM license ($10,000–$60,000 / product line).

### Tier 3: Custom Typefoundry Engineering & Bespoke Brand Cuts
- **Target**: Health-tech startups, pharmaceutical branding, medical packaging designers.
- **Deliverables**:
  - **Custom Hospital & Clinic Glyphs**: Synthesis of proprietary hospital logos, custom dosage pictograms, or localized syllabic extensions into the font binary.
  - **Dedicated Variable Font Tuning**: Bespoke axis calibration (`wght`, `wdth`, `slnt`, `opsz`) tuned for specialized thermal label printers and curved blister packs.
  - **Private Hosted Edge CDN**: High-availability CDN delivery with 99.99% uptime guarantee.
- **Engagement**: Project-based retainer ($20,000–$80,000 / bespoke family).
